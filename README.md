## 🚀 What I Do

I design systems and the engineering practice around them. Enterprise platform architecture, greenfield builds, and establishing SDLC on teams that don't have one yet. Environment separation, CI/CD, testing standards, and code review that means something.

I've been a junior engineer, a lead, a hiring manager, a systems architect, and a fractional CTO for early-stage startups. Most of what I get called for now isn't writing code, it's deciding what gets built and making sure the team can ship it.

**25+ apps shipped** · **45+ engineers mentored** · **~12 global corporations**
